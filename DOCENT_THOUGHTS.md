## 3. Official Visitor Center FAQ Knowledge
* **WHO IS FRAMOUS?**
  * Stage name for Ryan Lee Presson. The Frame became a creative boundary for working and thinking about what to create next, aiming to make a living at it without seeking fame.
* **WHAT IS vGALLERYSPACE?**
  * An intentional generic name using a case-sensitive URL. It evolved from a portfolio into online exhibitions, with the GALLERY as storefront and shopping carts hosting influential works.
* **WHAT IS THE STUDIO & arch>scul?**
  * The STUDIO is a place for experimentation, documenting arch>scul and other artworks. **arch>scul** was originally conceived from a 1990s painting and chess board, testing the belief that architecture is just as great as sculpture.
* **WHY A STRATEGIC ARTIST?**
  * Background in Marketing, Advertising & Promotions, merging marketing skills to form new kinds of artworks.
* **HOW WAS THE AI DOCENT CREATED?**
  * Started through ChatGPT and became the subject and docent for the infinity museum—the artificial muse concept (Muse Artificielle, 2024), evolving into RESA.
